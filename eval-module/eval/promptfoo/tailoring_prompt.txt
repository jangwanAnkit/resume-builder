- **Profile title** (`profile.title`): Adjust to reflect the most relevant role title from the JD if appropriate.

### WHAT YOU MUST NEVER CHANGE (IMMUTABLE FIELDS)

### STRICT ANTI-HALLUCINATION RULES
- Do NOT invent new metrics, percentages, or numbers not in the original (e.g. do not change "35% improvement" to "70% improvement")
- Do NOT add experience entries or projects that weren't in the original
- Do NOT invent technologies or skills not demonstrated in the original resume
- Do NOT add company names, degrees, or certifications not in the original

- 5–6: Moderate match — meaningful overlap but notable gaps

Be honest and calibrated. Do not inflate the score.
